# NDI for Android

**NDI for Android** is an open-source .NET MAUI app that turns an Android phone or tablet into a
monitor, a PTZ camera controller and a wireless camera for an NDI® production network. It discovers
NDI sources on your local network, plays them back with audio and tally, controls PTZ cameras (NDI
and VISCA over IP), and can publish the device's screen, camera or microphone as an NDI source.
Nothing leaves your local network, and the app has no account, no cloud service and no analytics.
